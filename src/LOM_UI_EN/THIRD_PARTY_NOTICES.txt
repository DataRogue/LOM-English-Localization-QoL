Translation origin: this mod's English grew from Lash's English Patch by joshfreitas1984
(https://github.com/joshfreitas1984/LegendOfMortalOverLlm), a machine translation of the game. Since
0.6 every line has been reviewed in its scene and many rewritten; the reviewed lines that kept that
patch's wording, and the three layout files in translation/scene/resize/ (copies of that patch's
resizers), are that patch's work. When the patch is installed, the plugin also reads the player's own
copy at runtime (the fallback and the "Lash's" option); it never changes it.
